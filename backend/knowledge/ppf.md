# Public Provident Fund (PPF)

## Overview
A 15-year government-backed long-term savings scheme offering guaranteed returns and the highest tax efficiency (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt / EEE status).

## Key Parameters
- **Interest Rate**: Set quarterly by the Ministry of Finance (~7.1% p.a. compounded annually).
- **Contribution Limits**: Minimum ₹500/year, Maximum ₹1,50,000/year.
- **Tenure**: 15 years, extendable in blocks of 5 years with or without contributions.
- **Tax Status**: Contribution qualifies for Section 80C deduction, interest earned is 100% tax-free, and maturity amount is completely tax-free.

## Strategic Role
Excellent risk-free sovereign debt component in a retirement portfolio, shielding wealth from market drawdowns and creditors (PPF cannot be attached by court decree).
