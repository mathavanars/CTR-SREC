# Emergency Fund Essentials

## Definition
An emergency fund is a liquid financial reserve reserved exclusively for unexpected life events: medical emergencies, sudden job loss, urgent home or vehicle repairs, or family contingencies.

## Sizing the Fund
- **Single / Salaried with steady employment**: 6 months of mandatory living expenses (fixed EMIs, rent, bills, basic food).
- **With Dependents / Irregular Freelance / Business income**: 9 to 12 months of mandatory expenses.

## Liquid Parking Strategy
Never invest emergency funds in equity or illiquid lock-in products.
- 30% in instant-access savings account (UPI / ATM ready).
- 40% in Overnight / Liquid Mutual Funds (T+1 day redemptions).
- 30% in Sweep-in Bank Fixed Deposit.
