# Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)

## What is an SIP?
A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a method of investing a fixed sum of money at regular intervals (typically monthly) into mutual funds.

## Key Benefits
1. **Rupee Cost Averaging**: When markets are down, your fixed amount buys more units; when markets are up, it buys fewer units, averaging your purchase cost over cycles.
2. **Disciplined Habit**: Eliminates emotional market timing and induces automated financial discipline.
3. **Power of Compounding**: Regular monthly investments over 10-20 years allow interest on interest to compound exponentially.

## Best Practice for Indian Investors
- Schedule SIP deduction within 3 to 5 days after monthly salary credit.
- Choose broad market index funds (Nifty 50) rather than chasing last year's top thematic fund.
- Step up your SIP by 10% annually whenever you receive an increment or salary hike.
