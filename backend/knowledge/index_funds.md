# Index Funds & Passive Equity

## What are Index Funds?
Index funds replicate a market benchmark like the Nifty 50 or BSE Sensex by holding the exact same shares in the same proportion.

## Why Index Funds for First-Time Investors?
1. **Low Expense Ratio**: Direct index fund expense ratios are typically 0.10% to 0.20%, compared to 1.0% - 2.0% for active funds.
2. **Zero Fund Manager Risk**: Over 80% of active large-cap funds in India fail to beat their benchmark index over 5 and 10-year horizons (SPIVA India report).
3. **Broad Economic Participation**: Buying Nifty 50 means owning the top 50 revenue-generating enterprises driving the Indian economy.

## Expected Long-Term Compounding
Historically in India, broad equity indices have returned approximately 11% to 13% CAGR over 15-20 year rolling periods, outperforming 6% inflation.
