# Indian Personal Taxation Basics

## Old Tax Regime vs New Tax Regime
- **New Tax Regime (Default from FY 2023-24)**: Lower tax slab rates, Standard deduction of ₹75,000 for salaried employees. Rebate under Section 87A makes taxable income up to ₹7 Lakh completely tax-free (effective ₹7.75 Lakh with standard deduction). Most deductions (80C, 80D, HRA) are not allowed.
- **Old Tax Regime**: Higher slab rates, but permits major deductions:
  - Section 80C: Up to ₹1.5 Lakh (EPF, PPF, ELSS, Home loan principal).
  - Section 80D: Up to ₹25,000 for self/family health insurance (+ ₹50,000 for senior citizen parents).
  - Section 80CCD(1B): Up to ₹50,000 additional for NPS.
  - Section 24(b): Up to ₹2,00,000 interest deduction on home loan for self-occupied property.
  - HRA exemption under Section 10(13A).

## Capital Gains Tax (Finance Act 2024 updates)
- **Listed Equity / Equity Mutual Funds**:
  - Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG, held < 12 months): 20%
  - Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG, held >= 12 months): 12.5% on gains exceeding ₹1.25 Lakh per financial year.
- **Debt Funds / Bank FDs**: Taxed at applicable personal income tax slab rates.
