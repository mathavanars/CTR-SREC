# National Pension System (NPS)

## Overview
A voluntary defined-contribution pension system regulated by PFRDA, designed to inculcate retirement savings among Indian citizens.

## Asset Allocation in NPS
Contributions can be split across four asset classes:
- **Asset Class E (Equity)**: Up to 75% in domestic equities.
- **Asset Class C (Corporate Debt)**: Investment grade bonds.
- **Asset Class G (Government Securities)**: Central and state government bonds.
- **Asset Class A (Alternative Assets)**: REITs, InvITs (up to 5%).

## Tax Deductions
- **Section 80CCD(1)**: Within overall ₹1.5 Lakh 80C limit.
- **Section 80CCD(1B)**: Additional exclusive deduction up to ₹50,000/year over and above 80C.
- **Maturity**: At age 60, up to 60% can be withdrawn tax-free as a lump sum. At least 40% must be used to purchase an annuity (regular pension).
