# Gold as a Portfolio Asset

## Role of Gold
Gold is not a productive cashflow-generating asset (like stocks or real estate), but it serves as:
1. **Inflation Hedge**: Long-term preserver of purchasing power.
2. **Crisis Hedge**: Tends to appreciate during geopolitical conflicts, global financial crises, and currency devaluation.
3. **Low Correlation**: Balances domestic equity drawdowns.

## Recommended Allocation
- 5% to 10% of total portfolio value.
- Avoid physical jewelry for investment (incurs 10-25% making charges and GST).
- Preferred instruments: Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs) for 2.5% annual sovereign coupon + tax-free capital gains at maturity, or Gold ETFs / Gold Mutual Funds for liquidity.
