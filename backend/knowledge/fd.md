# Fixed Deposit (FD) & Debt Instruments

## What is a Bank Fixed Deposit?
A Fixed Deposit is a low-risk financial instrument provided by banks and NBFCs where money is locked for a specific tenure at a guaranteed interest rate.

## Returns & Safety
- **Expected Return**: Typically 6.5% to 7.5% per annum for regular citizens (extra 0.5% for senior citizens).
- **Deposit Insurance**: DICGC insures principal and interest up to ₹5 Lakh per bank per depositor in India.

## Tax Treatment
- Interest earned is added to income and taxed according to your personal income tax slab.
- TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) applies if interest exceeds ₹40,000/year (₹50,000 for senior citizens).

## Best Use Case
- Emergency fund auto-sweep deposits.
- Capital preservation for immediate financial goals (< 3 years away).
- NOT suitable for long-term retirement wealth because post-tax returns barely beat 6% inflation.
